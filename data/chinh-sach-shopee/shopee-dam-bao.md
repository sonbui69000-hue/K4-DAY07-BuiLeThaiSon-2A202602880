# Shopee Dam Bao va quyen tra hang hoan tien

## Pham vi bao ve

Shopee Dam Bao bao ve quyen loi cua nguoi mua bang co che tra hang hoan tien. Nguoi mua co the gui yeu cau tra hang hoan tien trong vong 15 ngay tinh tu khi don hang duoc cap nhat trang thai giao hang thanh cong.

## Truong hop chua nhan duoc hang

Neu don hang chua duoc chuan bi dung han, he thong Shopee co the huy don va hoan tien theo phuong thuc thanh toan cua don hang hoac vao So du TK Shopee. Neu don hang chua duoc giao dung han, nguoi mua co the gui yeu cau tra hang hoan tien voi ly do chua nhan duoc hang.

## Thoi gian Shopee phan hoi

Voi ly do chua nhan duoc hang, Shopee thong bao ket qua xu ly trong khoang 3-5 ngay lam viec.

## Sau khi bam Da nhan duoc hang

Nguoi mua van co the gui yeu cau tra hang hoan tien sau khi bam Da nhan duoc hang neu don hang con nam trong thoi han 15 ngay tinh tu trang thai giao hang thanh cong.
