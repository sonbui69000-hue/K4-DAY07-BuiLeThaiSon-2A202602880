# Quy dinh nguoi ban Shopee Mall ve hoan tien va phi phat sinh

## Thanh toan cho nguoi ban khi khong co khieu nai

Neu nguoi mua Shopee Mall xac nhan Da nhan duoc hang va khong co khieu nai hoac yeu cau tra hang hoan tien tai thoi diem xac nhan, khoan thanh toan cua don hang duoc chuyen vao So Du Tai Khoan Shopee cua nguoi ban Shopee Mall ngay sau khi nguoi mua bam xac nhan.

## Khi co yeu cau tra hang hoan tien

Neu nguoi mua Shopee Mall co khieu nai hoac yeu cau tra hang hoan tien truoc khi Shopee thanh toan cho nguoi ban, khoan thanh toan cua don hang se duoc Shopee tam giu cho den khi co ket luan cuoi cung. Neu Shopee chap nhan khieu nai hoac yeu cau tra hang hoan tien, Shopee co quyen hoan tien cho nguoi mua va tra lai hang hoa cho nguoi ban theo chinh sach Shopee Mall.

## Dieu chinh sau khi da thanh toan

Neu Shopee da thanh toan cho nguoi ban nhung sau do chap nhan khieu nai hoac yeu cau tra hang cua nguoi mua Shopee Mall, Shopee co quyen dieu chinh khoan thanh toan cua don hang de hoan lai cho nguoi mua.

## Phi phat sinh do loi cua nguoi ban

Nguoi ban Shopee Mall co the phai thanh toan phi phat sinh theo lan neu vi pham dieu khoan dich vu hoac chinh sach hien hanh. Vi du, huy don do het hang, khong xac nhan don dung han, hoac yeu cau nguoi mua huy don co muc phi 196.360 VND cho moi don hang bi huy. Huy don do dang sai gia co muc phi 490.910 VND cho moi don hang bi sai gia.

## Thoi han de nghi xem xet lai phi

Trong vong 14 ngay ke tu khi nguoi ban nhan thong bao thu phi phat sinh theo lan, hoac tu khi thanh toan phi phat sinh theo lan cho Shopee, nguoi ban Shopee Mall co the de nghi Shopee xem xet lai viec thu phi.
