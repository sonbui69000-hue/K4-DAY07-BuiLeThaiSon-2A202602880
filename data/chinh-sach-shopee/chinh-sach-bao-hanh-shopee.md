# Chinh sach bao hanh tren Shopee

## Trach nhiem cua nguoi ban

Nguoi ban co trach nhiem tiep nhan bao hanh san pham hoac dich vu cho nguoi mua theo cam ket trong chinh sach bao hanh cua nguoi ban hoac nha san xuat. Thong tin ve chinh sach bao hanh can duoc dang tai trong phan mo ta san pham hoac dich vu tren san Shopee.

## Quyen cua nguoi mua

Nguoi mua co quyen yeu cau bao hanh theo chinh sach bao hanh cua nguoi ban hoac nha san xuat khi dap ung dieu kien bao hanh da cong bo. Nguoi mua cung co quyen khieu nai hoac khieu kien nguoi ban neu nguoi ban tu choi bao hanh khong phu hop voi chinh sach da cong bo.

## Gioi han trach nhiem cua Shopee

Shopee khuyen cao nguoi mua kiem tra chinh sach bao hanh va bao tri truoc khi dat hang. Shopee khong phai la ben thuc hien nghia vu bao hanh cho san pham duoc dang ban, tru truong hop san pham do chinh Shopee dang ban. Shopee chi ho tro trong pham vi cho phep de san pham cua nguoi mua duoc bao hanh theo che do cua nguoi ban.

## Dieu kien bao hanh co ban

Dieu kien bao hanh co ban thuong gom: san pham con thoi han bao hanh, con tem hoac phieu bao hanh, va loi ky thuat khong phai do nguoi mua gay ra.

## Truong hop co the phat sinh chi phi

Nguoi mua co the phai chiu chi phi neu san pham het thoi han bao hanh, bi be vo, bien dang, chay no, am thap trong dong co do nguoi su dung, hoac thuoc cac truong hop khac theo chinh sach bao hanh da cong bo cua nguoi ban hoac nha san xuat.
